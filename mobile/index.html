No. Mohanji's teaching is universal — it asks only that you be honest with yourself.
How much time does it take?
The Daily Sadhana audio is 28 minutes. That is enough. Begin there.

The Spiritual Practices page lists everything — open it, choose one, begin tomorrow.
Is anything paid?
The core meditations and chants are free forever. Some advanced workshops carry a fee.
